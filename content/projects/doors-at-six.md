A friend organizing a student reading asked me to put the evening on a quarter sheet. Her running order began at 6:30. The person setting out chairs had been told six. Both had the right time. I wrote the two things that actually happened: “Doors open 6:00. First reader 6:30.” The organizer checked the times and reader order; I wrote, folded and copied the schedule.
